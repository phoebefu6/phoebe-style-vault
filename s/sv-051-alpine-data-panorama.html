Alpine Data Panorama

SV-051

Example: The data science lifecycle, set in Alpine Data Panorama

In plain words

A painted mountain panorama in the manner of old tourist relief maps, with real terrain as the canvas and fine, thin data lines drawn lightly over it in two or three inks on paper-toned ground. It feels calm and handsome rather than technical. The style is the delicate data layer resting on a painted landscape without shouting over it. Best for: market expansion map; supply chain risk; strategy journey storytelling; climate and environment data. Avoid: text or lettering generated inside the image.

Topics it fits

  • Market expansion mapthin route lines over painted terrain show where the business is heading across real geography
  • Supply chain riskhairline paths through mountain passes make bottlenecks and chokepoints read as places, not abstractions
  • Strategy journey storytellingthe panorama view lets a multi-year plan feel like a climb with visible stages
  • Climate and environment dataglacier or migration lines on painted relief keep the science accurate and the picture beautiful

Art style and layout

  • core locked [terrain_relief_is_the_canvas, data_as_delicate_overlay_linework, two_to_three_ink_palette, paper_tone_ground]
  • era reference 19th-century alpine panorama maps and tourist relief panoramas - never a named living artist

What must stay, what may change

subject - any geodata story with terrain (glaciers, migration, supply routes); the panorama-painting register stays
