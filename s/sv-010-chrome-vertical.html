Chrome Vertical

SV-010

Example: The data science lifecycle, set in Chrome Vertical

In plain words

A tiny metallic car races down a black glowing road into a towering city of dark, heavily textured structures, with a pale blue sky far above. It feels monumental, cold, fast and a little heroic. What makes it this style is the steep perspective: one small bright traveller dwarfed by a giant vertical world. Best for: aI transformation launch; scaling up keynote; speed to market hook; startup versus incumbents. Avoid: text or lettering generated inside the image.

Topics it fits

  • AI transformation launcha single bright vehicle racing into a towering world turns the move into a bold journey
  • Scaling up keynotethe steep vertical perspective makes growth feel enormous and urgent
  • Speed to market hookthe glowing road lines pull the eye forward like a progress bar
  • Startup versus incumbentsone small coloured traveller against dark grey giants sets up the underdog story in one glance

Art style and layout

  • Subject one small lone traveller dwarfed by a monumental vertical world it is racing into
  • Composition steep exaggerated perspective for height and speed, traveller low and centred in the foreground, structures towering out of frame
  • Light the road's own glowing white lines are the light source, taillights burning, daylight only in the far sky
  • Palette near-black ground, dense dark grey structure, one saturated accent (the vehicle), clear pale blue sky
  • Material/texture heavily textured gothic stone, black wet-reflective asphalt, polished metallic paint
  • Mood monumental, cold, forward, a little heroic
  • Camera/render low wide angle, crisp render, deep focus, no atmospheric haze softening the structures

What must stay, what may change
