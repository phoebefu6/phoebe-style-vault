Piped Devotion

SV-064

No finished piece in this style yet - the bands are its palette.

In plain words

A portrait of an ordinary working person - a bus driver, a nurse, a street sweeper - decorated like a holy icon, with every line piped like icing and beads pressed into the surface right to the edges. It feels devoted and tender, and treats someone usually ignored as worthy of reverence. What makes it this style is the confectioner's piped rope line given, completely straight, to an everyday sitter.

Art style and layout

  • core locked [piped_rope_not_stroke, beads_are_embedded, ornament_to_all_four_edges, zero_neutrals, ordinary_sitter]
  • the tension a confectioner's technique spent on a person who never gets painted, held completely straight. If the sitter reads as glamorous the gap closes and the picture becomes decor; if the technique reads as a joke the reverence dies
  • piped rope not stroke THE CARD, and the first thing MJ will break. Its reflex is thick impasto or a flat graphic line. Say "squeezed from a nozzle as a raised round rope that holds its own shape and casts its own shadow" every time. A brushstroke anywhere in the frame is a miss
  • beads are embedded pressed physically into the wet ropes so they sit proud - not printed dots, not painted highlights, not glitter texture. If the specular hits are soft, MJ has painted them instead of embedding them
  • ordinary sitter the load-bearing constraint and the easiest to lose. A bus driver, not a queen. MJ will drift toward fashion editorial the moment the sitter is unspecified
  • ornament to all four edges no empty ground. The piped scrollwork runs off every edge, which is what makes it an icon rather than a portrait on a coloured backdrop
  • zero neutrals darks are deep saturated hues. A grey shadow anywhere flattens the whole card into ordinary illustration
  • palette [persimmon "#E2542A, cherry red "#C81127, butter yellow "#F2D25C, plum noir "#3B1436, wasabi "#8C9A3B on a ground around "#2B0E2E; specular hits pure white but only as pinpoints]
  • negative space none, deliberately. The eye rests on the sitter's face and nowhere else
  • era reference votive and ex-voto painting, folk reliquary, contemporary bead-and-relief figuration. Do not name a living artist and do not borrow a specific cultural tradition's motifs - see Card notes

What must stay, what may change

subject - ten far-apart SITTERS given the same devotional treatment (a bus driver, a grandmother at a stove, a boxer, a nurse on a night shift, a market fishmonger, a boy with a kite, a welder, a midwife, a street sweeper, a woman asleep on a train). NOT ten glamorous figures - the card only works when the sitter is ordinary and the treatment is not. The piped line and the embedded gems stay locked across all ten
