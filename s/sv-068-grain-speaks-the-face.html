Grain Speaks The Face

SV-068

No finished piece in this style yet - the bands are its palette.

In plain words

A large human face carved in shallow relief into plain timber, where the natural wood grain itself draws the wrinkles, cheeks and expression, knots and cracks left in. It feels warm, honest and weathered. What makes it this style is that the grain does the drawing - there is no carved outline.

Topics it fits

  • Leadership and character storiesa face drawn by the wood's own grain suggests character formed slowly, by what happened to you
  • Customer and employee portraitskeeping the defects in gives each person a dignified, unretouched look
  • Personal brand about rootsindustrial lumber shaped into a specific expression ties a personal story to plain, real material
  • Workshop themes on authenticityvisible knots and cracks are a direct visual for not hiding flaws

Art style and layout

  • core locked [grain_does_the_drawing, no_carved_outline, flat_daylight, shallow_relief, defects_stay]
  • the tension industrial lumber against a specific human expression, and enormous scale against
  • grain does the drawing THE CARD, and the first thing MJ will break. Its reflex is to carve
  • flat daylight one even overcast source, frontal, never raking. This is the opposite instruction
  • shallow relief no more than two centimetres. Deep carving becomes a sculpture with its own
  • defects stay knots, splits and checks must cross the face, not politely avoid it. A knot landing
  • palette [bleached sapwood "#E4D6BC, honey heartwood "#C09A63, grain line "#7A5A32, knot
  • negative space none. The panel is total and the crop is arbitrary
  • era reference devotional wood relief and contemporary monumental carving. **Do not name a living

What must stay, what may change

subject - ten far-apart FACES from ten different lives, one locked timber-relief language (a sleeping infant, a very old fisherman, a boxer mid-exhale, a nun, a teenager laughing, a stroke patient, a bride, a miner, a drowned saint, a newborn animal's face). The timber, the grain direction, the shallow depth and the flat daylight never move. Never ten serene adults - the emotional range IS the sweep
