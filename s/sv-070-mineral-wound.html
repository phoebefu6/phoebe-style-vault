Mineral Wound

SV-070

No finished piece in this style yet - the bands are its palette.

In plain words

A soft everyday object - a heart, a peach, a fist - cast in dark matte bronze, split open, with raw glowing crystals growing out of the break against a black void. It feels heavy, wounded and precious at the same time. What makes it this style is that the crack is the subject and the crystal is the only light.

Topics it fits

  • Growth after failurecrystals growing from the break show something valuable coming out of the damage itself
  • Resilience and leadership essaysa permanent bronze body still splitting open makes strength and hurt visible in one object
  • Personal brand stories of a career turnone object, one break, one light gives a single striking image for a turning-point post
  • Workshop themes on learning from incidentsthe wound as the focus invites a team to look at the break rather than hide it

Art style and layout

  • core locked [break_is_the_subject, crystal_is_the_only_light, both_materials_matte, void_ground, one_object_only]
  • the tension soft against permanent, and jewel against wound. It works only while the crystal reads as *growing* - as an intrusion with its own intent. The moment it reads as inlaid decoration the object becomes jewellery and the card dies
  • break is the subject ask for split open along one clean break, growing outward from the exposed interior. MJ's reflex is to give a whole intact object with crystals stuck on the outside, which is a completely different and much lesser picture. Check every frame for whether the mineral comes from *inside*
  • crystal is the only light the patina must absorb, the mineral must return. If both surfaces glint, the two materials have collapsed into one and the picture has no argument
  • both materials matte dull matte patina, raw unpolished, uncut. Polish is the failure mode here - it walks straight into the tasteful-luxury register the taste profile says she rejects fastest, and into SV-045's measured 2/5
  • void ground black seamless, no surface, no horizon, no shadow on a floor. The object floats. A table introduces still-life conventions and a second subject
  • palette [patina "#1F2A22, bronze "#5A4632, crystal violet "#8E6FC4 to clear "#DCD6EE, void "#080808]
  • negative space total and black. The object is an island
  • era reference reliquary and memento mori, crossed with mineral specimen photography. Do not name a living artist - the source stays out of every prompt

What must stay, what may change

subject - ten far-apart SOFT BODIES cast in the same patinated bronze, each split open with the same raw crystal growth erupting from the cut (a human heart, a peach, a clenched fist, a sea urchin, a loaf of bread, a bird's nest, a wasp comb, a folded pair of lungs, a pomegranate, a boxing glove). The metal, the crystal, the light and the staging never move. Ten different categories of soft thing - never ten organs and never ten fruits
