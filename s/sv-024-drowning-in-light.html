Drowning In Light

SV-024

Example: The data science lifecycle, set in Drowning In Light

In plain words

A face or form is cropped so close it stops being a portrait, wet and backlit with warm amber light against cool teal, every droplet sparkling and the highlights almost blown out. It feels submerged, breath-held and sensual. What makes it this style is the light itself: the subject drowns in it. Best for: information overload; burnout and recovery stories; personal brand reflection posts; deep focus and flow. Avoid: never ask for a *count* of sparkles - a count makes MJ multiply objects; text or lettering generated inside the image.

Topics it fits

  • Information overloada subject drowning in light shows what too much input feels like
  • Burnout and recovery storiesthe breath-held, submerged mood lets a personal story be felt before it is explained
  • Personal brand reflection poststhe extreme close crop creates intimacy and honesty
  • Deep focus and flowwarm light pouring over a close face captures being fully absorbed in work

Art style and layout

  • Subject a face or form, wet, cropped close enough that it stops being a portrait
  • Composition extreme close crop, subject cut by the frame on three sides, wet strands crossing diagonally, one sharp element near camera
  • Light hard warm backlight raking through a wet surface, cool teal fill from the front, halation around every highlight
  • Palette amber and copper against desaturated teal. Two temperatures, no third hue
  • Material/texture water on skin, wet hair, droplets reading as specular points, fine grain
  • Mood submerged, breath-held, sensual without posing
  • Camera/render cinematic macro, 85mm wide open, exposed to the edge of blowing out
