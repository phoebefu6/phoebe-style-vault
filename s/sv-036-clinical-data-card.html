Clinical Data Card

SV-036

No finished piece in this style yet - the bands are its palette.

In plain words

A white reference card with one realistic object or specimen at the top, thin pointer lines running out to labels, then a grid of small numbered cells below, closed by a bright coloured strip at the bottom. It feels clinical and measured, like a medical or lab handout. The style is the mix of one rendered hero and an orderly numbered grid.

Topics it fits

  • Model card for an AI systemthe numbered chip grid lays out purpose, data, limits and risks as a reference someone can check line by line
  • KPI definitions sheetuniform numbered cells give each metric the same space so none can hide
  • Risk register summarycolour pairs that encode severity plus a closing top-N band make the biggest risks the loudest strip
  • Anatomy of a data productpointer lines from a rendered object to labels show what each part does with lab-grade authority

Art style and layout

  • core locked [ground, hero_treatment, chip_grid, semantic_colour_pairs, accent_footer_band]
  • ground pure white to near-white (#ffffff - #fcfcfa). NOT tinted, NOT paper-aged - clinical cleanliness is half the authority
  • hero treatment SIGNATURE: one photoreal or scientific-illustration subject, cut out on white, upper third, with hairline leader lines running to labels. The subject is rendered (studio-lit photo cutout, anatomical plate, technical render) - never flat vector, never diagram boxes
  • chip grid SIGNATURE: the body is a grid of discrete numbered cells. Each = circled numeral + icon + bold short label + 1-2 lines of micro-copy. Uniform cell size. This is what makes it scan as a reference card rather than a poster
  • semantic colour pairs 2-3 hue families, each used as a light tint fill + saturated stroke/text of the SAME hue. Colour always encodes a category, never decorates. One hue per meaning + a legend
  • rating encodings small horizontal bars, filled-dot scales or star rows for quantified attributes - always with the scale stated
  • accent footer band SIGNATURE: a saturated full-width strip closing the sheet, carrying a top-N list or the one-line summary. The only large area of saturated colour on the page
  • leader lines hairline (0.5-0.75px), single weight, right-angle or gentle diagonal, terminating in a small dot at the subject
  • density information-maximal. Tiny body type (6.5-8px at A4), near-zero dead space. Built to be zoomed, not glanced
  • typography heavy grotesque sans display; small sans body; circled numerals; mono reserved for codes and units
  • palette [white "#ffffff, ink "#1a1a1a, grey "#6b7280, plus 2-3 semantic hue families per sheet]
  • mood authoritative, reference-grade, "someone measured this
  • era reference field-guide plate meets modern medical patient-education infographic; Dorling Kindsley density with contemporary flat-UI colour discipline
  • character tunable - any subject that can be rendered as a single physical object
  • context environment tunable - cut out on white is the default; a thin environment photo band behind the hero is permitted

What must stay, what may change

Swap the subject and the hue families freely. Keep the white clinical ground, the rendered hero with hairline leader lines, the uniform numbered chip grid, tint+stroke colour pairs that encode meaning, and the saturated footer band. The moment the hero becomes a flat vector diagram, this style is gone.
