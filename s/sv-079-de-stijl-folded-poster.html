De Stijl Folded Poster

SV-079

Example: The data science lifecycle, set in De Stijl Folded Poster

In plain words

A vintage folded poster on aged off-white paper with crease shadows, text set bold and left-aligned on the left two-thirds, and blocks of red, blue and yellow with thick black lines stacked on the right. It feels rational, modernist and a little archival. What makes it this style is the uneven grid of primary-colour blocks against tight black type on old paper. Best for: history, origins, context and timeline pages - in her words, like a page from an old magic book. Avoid: KPI, decision and form-heavy pages, where blocks without arrows hide the logic.

Topics it fits

  • History of AIthe archival folded-paper look gives a timeline of how AI developed a sense of real history
  • Origins of a data programmean old modernist poster frames where an initiative came from with authority and calm order
  • Strategic context openerthe left-hand text and right-hand blocks read as a disciplined board-level backgrounder
  • Milestone launch postersbold primary blocks and tight sans type give an announcement keynote punch while staying orderly

Art style and layout

  • core locked [asymmetric_mondrian_grid, primary_geometric_blocks, thick_black_rules, bold_tight_sans, aged_folded_paper]
  • ground aged off-white #E8E4DB, fold creases (one vertical, one horizontal), soft crease shadows, fibre texture
  • rules thick black bars, edges slightly bled
  • blocks circle, big square, small square, half-circle, parallel bars; flat fills, no gradients
  • type bold tight left-aligned sans headline; small vermilion subtitle; body #333
  • colour roles (source) red, deep blue, yellow, black
  • the tension strict geometry against a worn, folded, hand-printed sheet. Too clean and it is a template; too worn and it is a costume.

What must stay, what may change

Swap the subject freely. Keep the asymmetric Mondrian grid (text about two-thirds left, read in an F-pattern; primary geometric blocks stacked on the right), thick slightly bled black rules, tight bold left-aligned industrial sans, aged off-white folded paper with crease shadows.
