Unit Translation

SV-055

No finished piece in this style yet.

In plain words

A plain, almost technical drawing that takes a number people cannot feel - a few milliseconds, a fraction of a percent - and shows it as a real distance or object at true scale. It feels calm and exact, and the surprise comes from how big or small the thing turns out to be. The rule that makes it this style is honesty: the scale is never stretched for drama.

Topics it fits

  • Latency and performance SLAsturning milliseconds into metres a user would walk makes a tiny delay feel physical to a non-technical board
  • Error rates and model accuracyre-expressing 0.5 percent as a true-scale crowd or shelf shows what a small rate means in real cases
  • Cost per transactiona drawn-to-scale physical unit lets executives compare costs with their body, not a spreadsheet
  • KPI gaps against targetthe dual top-down and perspective view makes a narrow miss visible without exaggerating it

Art style and layout

  • core locked [abstract_quantity_re_expressed_physically, drawn_to_true_scale, dual_view_topdown_plus_perspective, minimal_track_furniture]
  • the tension the honesty constraint IS the style - the moment the scale is exaggerated for drama, the piece has no reason to exist

What must stay, what may change

subject - any abstract gap (latency, cost, error rates) re-expressed in a physical unit the body understands
