Soft Data Organisms

SV-049

Example: The data science lifecycle, set in Soft Data Organisms

In plain words

Soft glowing blob-like shapes float in dark space, joined by hair-thin lines and labelled in tiny careful type, with no axes anywhere. It looks like living cells or deep-sea creatures. The style uses organic, lopsided forms to make uncomfortable data feel felt rather than neutral. Best for: inequality and fairness in AI; customer churn and loss; mental health in tech culture; gallery data art. Avoid: text or lettering generated inside the image.

Topics it fits

  • Inequality and fairness in AIlopsided organic shapes make an unequal outcome feel like a living imbalance rather than a cold bar
  • Customer churn and losssoft bodies drifting apart on hairline connectors give a story of loss its proper weight
  • Mental health in tech culturethe tender, glowing forms suit a sensitive topic that hard charts would flatten
  • Gallery data arttiny labels on luminous organisms turn a real dataset into a piece people linger over

Art style and layout

  • core locked [organic_blob_and_filament_vocabulary, hairline_connectors, tiny_annotation_labels, dark_ground, asymmetry_as_argument]
  • palette near-black ground, soft luminous body colours per entity, hairline white/grey connectors
  • composition layered organisms floating in dark space; no axes visible - structure carried by connection lines and micro-labels

What must stay, what may change

subject - any uncomfortable dataset (inequality, risk, loss); the organic non-neutral form language stays
