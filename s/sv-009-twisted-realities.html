Twisted Realities

SV-009

Example: The data science lifecycle, set in Twisted Realities

In plain words

An impossible building or object that almost makes sense, photographed like real architecture, with perspective pushed too far and two light sources that disagree. It feels like a calm dream, not a nightmare. What makes it this style is real-looking surfaces on a structure that cannot exist. Best for: messy data pipelines; assumptions that do not add up; design thinking workshop; career paths that are not linear. Avoid: text or lettering generated inside the image.

Topics it fits

  • Messy data pipelinesan impossible structure that almost works is a fair picture of a system held together by workarounds
  • Assumptions that do not add uptwo contradicting light sources quietly show a story with conflicting evidence
  • Design thinking workshopa dream-like but plausible building nudges people to question what they take as fixed
  • Career paths that are not lineara staircase that bends somewhere unexpected is a gentle way to tell a non-obvious journey

Art style and layout

  • Subject an impossible architecture or object that almost makes sense
  • Composition one-point perspective pushed too far, horizon where it should not be
  • Light two light sources that contradict each other
  • Palette plausible until you look - a sky-colour on a floor
  • Material/texture convincingly real surfaces on an unreal structure
  • Mood calm dream, not nightmare
  • Camera/render architectural photography, wide, tripod-still

What must stay, what may change

composition (ten impossible geometries)
