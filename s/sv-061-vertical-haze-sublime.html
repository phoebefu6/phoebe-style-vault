Vertical Haze Sublime

SV-061

Example: The data science lifecycle, set in Vertical Haze Sublime

In plain words

A tiny dark figure seen from behind, standing in front of an enormous glass-and-steel system that disappears into blue fog, lit only by amber windows and screens far away. It feels grand, cold and a little overwhelming. What makes it this style is that the only living thing in the picture is the only thing with no light on it. Best for: keynote openers on AI scale; data platform launches; the human in the loop; future of work talks. Avoid: text or lettering generated inside the image.

Topics it fits

  • Keynote openers on AI scaleone unlit person against an endless lit system shows the size of the change before a word is spoken
  • Data platform launchesstrong vertical columns and layers of haze make infrastructure look like architecture worth announcing
  • The human in the loopputting the person in shadow while the system glows makes the audience ask who is really in charge
  • Future of work talksthe tiny figure facing a structure, not a landscape, frames the hook question of where people fit

Art style and layout

  • Subject one human, unlit, at roughly 5% of the frame height, seen from behind, dwarfed by a
  • Composition cinematic wide (~1.85:1), near-symmetrical, with a **strong vertical rhythm of glass
  • Light there is no key light on the subject. Every light source is behind or inside the
  • Palette near-monochrome desaturated slate blue / teal grey / near-black, with **sodium amber
  • Material/texture haze is the medium. Volumetric atmosphere carries the depth, not detail;
  • Mood awe, isolation, sublime dread. Sober, never menacing and never heroic. Nothing is exploding
  • Camera/render cinematic still, long lens compression, deep planes but soft, gentle bloom, fine

What must stay, what may change

subject - ten far-apart SYSTEMS a person stands too small in front of, NEVER ten cyberpunk cities
