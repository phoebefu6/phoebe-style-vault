Squish Still Life

SV-012

Example: The data science lifecycle, set in Squish Still Life

In plain words

An everyday object, like a stapler or a key, looks as if it is made of soft inflated silicone, with seams and pinches, sitting on a pastel stand under soft studio light. It feels cute, absurd and very touchable. What makes it this style is turning hard, familiar things soft and squishy. Best for: making AI feel friendly; onboarding and first-day guides; workplace culture posts; workshop icebreakers. Avoid: text or lettering generated inside the image.

Topics it fits

  • Making AI feel friendlya soft squishy version of a familiar tool lowers the fear around new technology
  • Onboarding and first-day guidesthe cute, touchable objects make dry instructions feel welcoming
  • Workplace culture postsan inflated office stapler gently pokes fun at office life without being harsh
  • Workshop icebreakersthe absurd soft objects get a laugh and loosen up a room

Art style and layout

  • Subject an everyday object rendered as if inflated or upholstered - a stapler, a book, a key, a chair, made of soft matter
  • Composition single object or a tight pair on a plinth, centred, shallow stage
  • Light broad soft studio light, one gentle contact shadow, no specular hotspot
  • Palette pastel two-tone ground with the object one shade off it - sherbet, putty, dusty mint
  • Material/texture matte silicone, brushed felt, inflated vinyl, visible seam and pinch
  • Mood cute, absurd, touchable
  • Camera/render clean 3D product render, 50mm, slight top-down

What must stay, what may change

subject (ten office or household objects, one material locked)
